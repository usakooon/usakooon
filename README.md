## Research

### [Walkability for Elderly People in Hilly and Flat Station Areas](https://github.com/usakooon/research-portfolio)

I compare walkability around Shin-Yurigaoka and Nagareyama-Otakanomori stations in Japan, combining Space Syntax, slope analysis and field surveys with QGIS and OpenStreetMap.

Presented at **FOSS4G Hiroshima 2026, Japan — Academic Track**.
